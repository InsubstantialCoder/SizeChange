Plugin for ffxiv that scales character models based on their health. Based on Shrinkage by mikelosx - https://github.com/mikelosx/Shrinkage

There are slash commands now!
/scale <num> [num]

This will change your scale to the provided number or a random number between the two provided numbers (if you use two numbers).
For example:
/scale 2
sets you to scale 2

/scale 1 70
sets you to a random scale between 1 and 70

/grow <num> [num]
increases your scale by num percent or a random value if you provide two numbers.
/grow 1 100
increases your scale by between 1 and 100 percent

/shrink works the same way only in reverse.
